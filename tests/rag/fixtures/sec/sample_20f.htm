Item 4. Information on the Company

The company is a leading semiconductor manufacturer with operations spanning multiple continents. Founded in 1987, the company has grown to become one of the world's largest contract chipmakers. The company operates advanced fabrication facilities producing cutting-edge process nodes. Key products include logic chips, mixed-signal semiconductors, and advanced packaging solutions. The company serves a diverse customer base across consumer electronics, automotive, and data center markets.

Item 5. Operating and Financial Review

Revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 increased 25% year-over-year driven by strong demand for advanced process nodes. Gross margin expanded to 58.5% from 55.2% in the prior year reflecting improved capacity utilization and favorable product mix. Operating expenses increased 12% reflecting continued investment in research and development. Net income grew 30% to $12.5 billion. The company generated $18.2 billion in operating cash flow and invested $15.8 billion in capital expenditures to expand advanced node capacity.
