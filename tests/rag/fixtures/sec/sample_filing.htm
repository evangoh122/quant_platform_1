Item 1. Business

NVIDIA Corporation is a leading designer of graphics processing units (GPUs) for the gaming, professional visualization, data center, and automotive markets. The company's products include GPUs, system-on-chip units (SoCs), and automotive platforms. NVIDIA's GPU products are used in gaming, professional visualization, data center, and automotive markets.

The company was incorporated in California in 1993 and reincorporated in Delaware in 1998. NVIDIA's headquarters are in Santa Clara, California.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to numerous risks and uncertainties, including those described below. These risks could materially affect our business, financial condition, or results of operations. The COVID-19 pandemic has created significant uncertainty and disruption in the global economy. Supply chain disruptions have affected our ability to obtain components and manufacture products.

Competition in the GPU market is intense, with major competitors including AMD and Intel. Our revenue is concentrated in the gaming and data center markets, making us vulnerable to changes in demand in these markets.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Revenue for fiscal year 2025 increased 126% year-over-year to $130.5 billion, driven by strong demand for our data center products. Data center revenue increased 142% year-over-year to $115.2 billion. Gaming revenue increased 55% year-over-year to $11.2 billion.

Gross margin for fiscal year 2025 was 73.5%, compared to 72.7% in the prior year. The increase was primarily due to a favorable shift in product mix toward higher-margin data center products.
